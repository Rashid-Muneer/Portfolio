AI Search & LLM Visibility

Get cited in ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini and Google’s AI Overviews, not just ranked in Google. The new front page of search is AI answers.

What it is

Search isn’t just ten blue links anymore. More and more customers ask ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini and Google’s AI Overviews, and those engines answer by citing a handful of trusted sources. AI Search Optimization (also called GEO / AEO, Generative or Answer Engine Optimization) makes sure your business is one of the sources they quote, with accurate facts and a clear link back to you.

What’s included

  • Entity & brand clarity so engines understand exactly who you are
  • Structured data, FAQ and answer formatting that LLMs can parse and quote
  • Content written to directly answer high-intent questions
  • llms.txt, robots and crawler access for AI bots (GPTBot, PerplexityBot, etc.)
  • Consistent facts across your site, GMB and citations to avoid AI confusion
  • Monitoring how AI engines describe and cite your business
