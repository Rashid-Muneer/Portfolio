AI Search Optimization to Get Your Business Cited in ChatGPT, Perplexity, and AI Overviews

The front page of search is no longer just Google. ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini, and Google AI Overviews now answer questions directly, and they cite sources. AI search optimization (also called generative engine optimization or GEO) makes sure your business is one of those sources.

What Is AI Search Optimization

AI search optimization, also known as generative engine optimization (GEO) or answer engine optimization (AEO), is the practice of structuring your website and content so AI systems cite your business when answering user questions. This goes beyond traditional local SEO. Large language models like ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini, and Google AI Overviews pull from web content that provides clear, structured, authoritative, and factual answers.

The businesses that get cited are not random. They have content structured with clean heading hierarchies, FAQ schema, entity-rich descriptions, factual claims with sources, and formats that AI systems can parse and trust. This site itself is built with AI search optimization from the ground up, including an llms.txt file, structured data on every page, and content written to be citable by answer engines.

What You Get with AI Search Optimization

  • Entity SEO strategy that establishes your business as a recognized entity across knowledge graphs
  • Structured data and schema markup optimized for AI parsing, including FAQ, HowTo, LocalBusiness, and Service schemas
  • Content restructuring with clear question-and-answer formats, comparison tables, and fact-dense paragraphs
  • llms.txt file creation to guide AI crawlers in understanding your business and services
  • Citation-ready content with sourced data points that AI systems trust and reference
  • AI visibility monitoring across ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini, and Google AI Overviews
  • Integration with GBP optimization and on-page SEO for compounding visibility across traditional and AI search
