What it is

Before spending a rupee on optimization, you should know exactly where you stand. My SEO audit is a deep, prioritized review of your site and local presence, technical health, on-page quality, Google Business Profile, citations, content gaps and competitor positioning, delivered as a plain-English roadmap of what to fix first for the biggest impact.

What’s included

  • Technical crawl: indexing, site speed, Core Web Vitals, 301/404 errors, schema
  • On-page review: titles, meta, headings, internal linking and content depth
  • Google Business Profile & local audit: categories, NAP, reviews, citations
  • Keyword & content gap analysis vs. your top competitors
  • Search Console & analytics review of current performance
  • Prioritized action plan, quick wins first, then bigger moves
