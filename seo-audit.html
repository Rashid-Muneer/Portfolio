SEO Audit

SEO Audit Services That Find What Holds Your Rankings Back

Before spending money on SEO, you need to know exactly where you stand. My SEO audit services give you a clear, prioritized picture of every technical, on-page, local, and competitive gap holding your site back, with a fix-it roadmap you can act on immediately.

What an SEO Audit Covers

An SEO audit is a systematic evaluation of your website and online presence to identify what prevents you from ranking higher. I examine four interconnected layers: technical SEO (crawlability, indexing, speed, Core Web Vitals), on-page SEO (titles, meta descriptions, headings, content quality, internal links), local SEO (GBP health, citations, NAP consistency, geo-grid rankings), and competitive positioning (what your top-ranking competitors do differently and where you can overtake them).

The output is not a 50-page PDF full of jargon. It is a prioritized action plan sorted by impact, telling you what to fix first, what to fix next, and what will move the needle most. This is the same audit framework I use when onboarding every client, and it is the foundation that leads to results like Ridgeline Exteriors going from a 13.6 average map rank to 4.2.

What You Get in the Audit Report

  • Technical crawl analysis covering indexing, site speed, Core Web Vitals, mobile usability, and structured data
  • On-page review of titles, meta descriptions, heading structure, content quality, and internal linking
  • Local SEO evaluation including GBP audit, citation check, NAP consistency, and service area optimization
  • Competitor gap analysis showing keyword, content, and backlink opportunities
  • Prioritized fix-it roadmap ranked by effort versus impact
  • 30-minute walkthrough call to discuss findings and next steps
