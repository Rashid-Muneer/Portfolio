SEO Content Writing That Ranks in Google and Gets Cited by AI

Content is not just about filling pages. Every piece I write is built around real keyword research and search intent, structured for featured snippets and FAQ schema, and formatted to be citable by AI answer engines like ChatGPT, Perplexity, and Gemini.

What SEO Content Writing Delivers

SEO content writing is the creation of web pages, blog posts, and landing pages that are strategically built to rank for specific search queries and convert visitors into leads. It combines keyword research, search intent analysis, on-page optimization, and compelling writing into content that serves both search engines and real people.

In 2026, content also needs to be structured for AI answer engines. ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini, and Google AI Overviews increasingly pull from content that provides clear, well-structured, factual answers. My AI search optimization approach is baked into every piece of content from the start.

What You Get with SEO Content Services

  • Blog posts targeting high-intent long-tail keywords with proper H1, H2, H3 hierarchy
  • Service pages and location pages optimized for both organic and Map Pack rankings
  • FAQ sections with FAQ schema markup that improve snippet eligibility and AI citability
  • Internal linking that distributes authority to your money pages
  • Data-backed claims with properly sourced statistics and industry references
  • Comparison tables, process sections, and structured formats that earn featured snippets
  • Topic cluster strategy so each piece of content strengthens the others
