Keyword Research Services That Uncover the Searches Worth Winning

Ranking for the wrong keywords is worse than not ranking at all. My keyword research services identify the high-intent, local, and near-me searches your customers actually use, then map them to pages prioritized by real opportunity and competition level.

Why Keyword Research Matters for Local Businesses

Keyword research is the foundation of every SEO decision. It determines which pages you build, what content you write, how you structure your on-page optimization, and which local markets you target first. Without proper keyword research, you risk investing time and money into content that targets terms nobody searches for, or worse, terms you cannot realistically rank for.

For local businesses, keyword research is especially critical because the search landscape is different from national SEO. Modifiers like city names, "near me," and service-specific terms create thousands of unique keyword combinations. The right ones drive calls and bookings; the wrong ones attract tire-kickers or no traffic at all.

What You Get with Keyword Research Services

  • Complete keyword inventory for your services and service areas using Ahrefs, Semrush, and Search Console
  • Search intent classification for every keyword (informational, commercial, transactional, navigational)
  • Keyword-to-page mapping that assigns primary and secondary keywords to each URL on your site
  • Cannibalization analysis to identify pages competing against each other for the same terms
  • Competitor keyword gap analysis showing terms your competitors rank for that you do not
  • Local keyword opportunities including city-plus-service combinations and near-me variations
  • Prioritization matrix scoring keywords by search volume, intent, competition, and current ranking position
