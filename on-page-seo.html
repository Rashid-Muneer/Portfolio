Make every page earn its rankings, titles, meta, headings, content structure and internal links optimized around real search intent.

What it is

On-page SEO is where intent meets structure. I optimize each important page so search engines understand exactly what it’s about and users find what they came for, sharpening titles and meta descriptions, structuring headings, improving content depth and readability, and building internal links that pass authority to the pages that matter most.

What’s included

  • Title tags & meta descriptions written for clicks and keywords
  • Heading structure (H1-H3) aligned to topic and intent
  • Content optimization for depth, readability and search intent
  • Internal linking strategy that spreads authority to key pages
  • Image alt text, URL structure and on-page schema
  • Search-intent mapping so each page targets the right query

FAQ

Common questions

On-page SEO is optimizing the elements on a page, title, meta description, headings, content, internal links, images and schema, so it ranks for its target keywords and matches what searchers want.

On-page SEO focuses on the content and structure of individual pages; technical SEO focuses on the site-wide foundation (speed, indexing, crawlability, schema) that lets those pages rank at all. They work together.

Usually your money pages, services, location pages and high-intent landing pages, plus any pages already ranking on page two that just need a push.
