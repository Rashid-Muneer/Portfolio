Competitor Analysis

SEO Competitor Analysis That Shows Exactly Where You Can Win

You cannot outrank competitors you do not understand. My SEO competitor analysis breaks down exactly why they rank above you, their keywords, content strategy, backlink profile, and Google Business Profile signals, and turns those insights into a plan to overtake them.

What SEO Competitor Analysis Reveals

SEO competitor analysis is the process of identifying your top-ranking competitors and reverse-engineering their strategy. I examine their keyword portfolio, content strategy, backlink profile, technical SEO setup, and local SEO signals to find the gaps and opportunities your business can exploit.

This is not a vanity exercise. Every insight maps directly to an action you can take. If a competitor ranks because they have better service page content, we improve yours. If they rank because of a stronger citation profile, we build yours. The goal is to close every gap systematically, starting with the changes that will move rankings fastest.

What You Get with Competitor Analysis

  • Top 5 to 10 competitor identification for your core services and locations
  • Keyword gap analysis showing terms competitors rank for that your site does not
  • Content gap analysis comparing page depth, topic coverage, and content formats
  • Backlink profile comparison identifying link sources you can replicate
  • Google Business Profile benchmarking including categories, review counts, posting frequency, and photo volume
  • On-page comparison of title tags, heading structure, and schema markup
  • Actionable opportunity matrix showing where you are closest to overtaking each competitor

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Your rankings do not exist in a vacuum. Whether you rank first or tenth depends entirely on what your competitors are doing. An SEO competitor analysis shows you exactly why they outrank you and identifies the specific gaps you need to close to overtake them. Without it, you are optimizing blind.

I typically analyze 5 to 10 competitors depending on your market. These are identified based on who actually ranks for your target keywords, not just who you consider a competitor in the offline market. Sometimes your biggest SEO competitors are businesses you have never heard of.

I recommend a full competitor analysis at the start of any SEO engagement and then a lighter refresh every 3 to 6 months. Local search is dynamic. Competitors launch new content, earn new links, and optimize their GBP listings. Regular monitoring ensures you stay ahead of changes rather than reacting to them.
