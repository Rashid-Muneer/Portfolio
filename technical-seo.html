Technical SEO

Technical SEO Services for Faster, Crawlable, and Indexable Websites

Your content cannot rank if search engines cannot crawl, index, or render it properly. My technical SEO services fix the foundation of your website so that every piece of content and every on-page optimization you invest in can actually perform.

What Technical SEO Covers

Technical SEO is the optimization of your website's infrastructure so search engines can efficiently crawl, index, render, and rank your pages. It includes site speed and Core Web Vitals, mobile usability, crawl budget management, XML sitemaps, robots.txt configuration, canonical tags, redirect chains, structured data (schema markup), HTTPS configuration, and JavaScript rendering issues.

Think of technical SEO as the plumbing behind the walls. You will not see it when it works, but when it breaks, everything suffers. Pages get deindexed, load slowly, or never get crawled in the first place. I have seen clients lose months of on-page work and content production because a single technical issue prevented Google from indexing their pages.

What You Get with Technical SEO Services

  • Comprehensive technical crawl using Screaming Frog, Search Console, and manual inspection
  • Core Web Vitals optimization for LCP, FID/INP, and CLS to meet Google’s performance thresholds
  • Indexing and crawlability fixes including sitemap optimization, robots.txt cleanup, and canonicalization
  • Redirect chain resolution and 404 error cleanup
  • Structured data implementation for local business, services, FAQ, breadcrumbs, and reviews
  • Mobile usability audit and fixes
  • Site speed optimization through image compression, lazy loading, render-blocking resource management
