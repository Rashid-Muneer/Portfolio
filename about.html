What I Do as a Freelance Local SEO Specialist

For 2+ years I have run agency-grade local SEO campaigns for service businesses across the US, UK, and Gulf. My clients include roofers, med spas, skincare clinics, security firms, and tax practices. The job is straightforward to describe and hard to do well: get your business found by the people nearby who are ready to buy, in Google Maps, the organic results, and increasingly in AI answer engines like ChatGPT, Perplexity, and Gemini.

I approach SEO from a data background (BS in Data Science), so I do not guess. I track geo-grid rankings point by point, monitor Search Console closely, and tie the work back to calls, bookings, and leads. When FaceBeautyScience came to me with a thin Google Business Profile, that data-driven mindset rebuilt it into 23,213 profile views and 6.23M search impressions.
