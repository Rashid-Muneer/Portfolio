What I do

For 2+ years I’ve run agency-grade campaigns for service businesses, roofers, med spas, skincare clinics, security firms and tax practices, across the US, UK and the Gulf. The job is simple to describe and hard to do well: get you found by the people nearby who are ready to buy, on Google’s Map Pack, the organic results, and increasingly in AI answers.

I come at SEO from a data background, finishing a BS in Data Science, so I don’t guess. I track geo-grid rankings point by point, watch Search Console closely, and tie the work back to calls, bookings and leads. When FaceBeautyScience came to me with a thin Google Business Profile, that mindset is what rebuilt it into 23,213 profile views and 6.23M search impressions.
