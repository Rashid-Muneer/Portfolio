Local SEO Services That Put Your Business in the Google Map Pack

What Local SEO Actually Involves

Local SEO is the practice of optimizing your online presence so your business ranks in Google Maps, the local 3-Pack, and location-based organic results. Nearly 46% of all Google searches have local intent, and 76% of people who search for something nearby visit a business within 24 hours. If your business serves a specific area and you are not investing in local SEO, you are invisible to the customers most ready to buy.

My approach covers the full local stack. I start with a comprehensive SEO audit, then build outward through GBP optimization, citation management, hyper-local landing pages, and on-page SEO improvements. For Ridgeline Exteriors, this system lifted average geo-grid map rank from 13.6 to 4.2 in a single month.
